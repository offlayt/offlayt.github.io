## Промпт

Use case: product-mockup.
Asset type: one transparent-background vintage camera cutout for an animated website loading screen.
Primary request: a beautiful small antique folding roll-film camera, circa 1930, aged dark walnut base, near-black textured leather bellows and body, large circular warm brass lens facing the viewer, tiny winding knob and simple folded viewfinder on top. Entire camera visible, freestanding object, no tripod, no hand.
Style: premium photorealistic 3D museum-object/product render, tactile materials, careful miniature detail and natural patina, matches a nearly-black vintage room portfolio with walnut television, brass accents and cream paper. Soft warm upper-left key light and restrained cool edge light for separation against a dark UI; readable silhouette at 240 pixels wide.
Composition: square canvas, nearly orthographic straight-on FRONT VIEW, very slight top visible, symmetrical front lens genuinely circular, front lens centered horizontally at 50% and around 58% vertically. Camera fills about 86% width, comfortable transparent margins all around. Lens diameter around 27% canvas width, deep dark glass center, clear brass rings. Large lens and compact broad body, realistic proportions, minimal perspective distortion so CSS shutter animation can overlay the glass.
Constraints: truly transparent alpha background. Only the camera cutout, with no cast shadow outside the object. No background, floor, room or pedestal. NO text, letters, digits, logos, labels, watermarks, decorative sparkle marks, flash, UI, cables or other props. Do not reproduce the site's PNG logo; this must be a tangible old photographic camera.
